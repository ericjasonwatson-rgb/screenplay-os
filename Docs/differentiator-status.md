## Website copy proposed after implementation review

Do not apply these edits until the matching acceptance tests and owner GUI review pass. The current website correctly labels the list “Coming next.”

1. Replace “Continuity bible: characters, locations and style carried into every generation” only when adopted entries are visibly listed in the exact-send preview for every shipped generation path and their persistence boundary is disclosed.
2. Replace “Generation into shots: images, video and temp voice…” with the narrower shipped claim “Generate image Takes from a grounded shot request, review the exact send, and choose the Take used in Edit” after A1/A2 pass. Keep video and voice in “Coming next” until real adapters and playback exist.
3. Move performer consent out of “Coming next” only after the owner verifies the current fail-closed UI path. Proposed claim: “Real-performer likeness and voice requests require a current recorded consent.”
4. Remove “Temp voice, music and effects lanes” from “Coming next” only after A3 supports imported clips, synchronized playback, persistence, and handoff.
5. Replace the current handoff wording only when selected take media and audio are actually referenced by export. Until then retain “timing placeholders, not linked footage.”
6. Do not mention Pro, Studio, founding pricing, perpetual fallback, license enforcement, or checkout until commerce enforcement has been separately reviewed and intentionally enabled.

## Gate before any sale

The product is not ready to charge from build 28. A1 through A5 need green automated tests and owner GUI acceptance. Commerce also needs offline license verification, immutable free-core rules, legal review, and a real merchant-of-record decision. `commerce.enforcement` must remain off until those gates are complete.

## Post-build-28 implementation under review

The source after build 28 now closes the audited A1–A5 code gaps: generated and imported images become durable shot Takes with provenance; Take selection remains undoable and non-destructive; Edit has synchronized imported dialogue/music/effects lanes and includes those references in EDL/FCPXML handoff; the continuity bible persists inside the document with sidecar migration; and Activity provides a project cost ledger plus CSV. Video generation, generated voice, linked video playback, shared Studio bibles/review, and disclosure reports remain unimplemented and must not be claimed. These changes have not been published or shipped and still require owner GUI acceptance.
