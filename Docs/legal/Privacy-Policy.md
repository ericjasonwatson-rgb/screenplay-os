ScreenplayOS is local first. Scripts, continuity data, boards, takes, imported audio, generation records, and cost ledgers stay on the user's Mac unless the user exports them, moves them, or explicitly sends reviewed content to an AI provider. ScreenplayOS currently has no product analytics or screenplay telemetry.

AI provider credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. The app does not put keys in project files, logs, prompts, provenance exports, or cost-ledger exports. For an optional generation request, the app displays the exact prompt and referenced project material before sending. The chosen provider then processes that data under its own privacy terms. Provider, model, prompt, timestamp, and cost information may be stored locally for provenance.

Signed license files contain a license identifier, tier, seat count, plan identifier, dates, and version entitlement. Offline verification requires no contact with Beaver Point. Future checkout and license issuance will be handled through a merchant of record and a ScreenplayOS fulfillment service; those systems will receive transaction and contact information needed to complete the purchase, prevent fraud, issue licenses, and keep accounting records.

The ScreenplayOS email list uses Buttondown. If you subscribe, Buttondown receives the email and related delivery/subscription data.
